Tidepool Tours

Guided sea-kayak tours from Falmouth, for complete beginners and confident paddlers alike.

Tours

  • Harbour Explorer — 2 hours, £45. Calm water, ideal for a first time.
  • Seal Coast — 4 hours, £85. Sea caves and a seal colony; some experience needed.
  • Sunset Paddle — 2.5 hours, £55. Summer evenings only.

What to bring

Clothes that can get wet, a change of clothes, and water. We provide wetsuits, buoyancy aids and dry bags.

Booking

Tours run April to October. Minimum age 12. Cancel up to 48 hours before for a full refund; tours cancelled for weather are always refunded.
