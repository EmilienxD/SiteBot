Brewing guides

Start with a 1:16 ratio — 1 g of coffee to 16 g of water — and adjust by taste.

Pour-over (V60)

22 g coffee, medium-fine grind, 350 g water at 94 °C. Bloom with 50 g for 40 seconds, then pour in slow circles to 350 g by 1:45. Total brew time should land between 3:00 and 3:30.

French press

30 g coffee, coarse grind, 500 g water just off the boil. Steep 4 minutes, break the crust, skim, and wait another 5 minutes before plunging gently. Waiting gives a cleaner cup.

AeroPress

15 g coffee, fine grind, 230 g water at 85 °C, inverted. Stir 10 times, steep 1:30, flip and press for 30 seconds.

Espresso

18 g in, 38 g out, in 27–32 seconds at 93 °C. Rest espresso roasts 7–10 days after the roast date for the best extraction.

Storing beans

Keep beans in the resealable bag, away from light and heat. Do not refrigerate. Coffee is at its best between 5 and 30 days after roasting.
