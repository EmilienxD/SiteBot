Coffee roasted on Tuesday, in your cup by Friday.

We are a six-person roastery on Commercial Street in Portland, Maine. Every bag is roasted to order in 12 kg batches and ships within 48 hours of roasting.

Visit the roastery

The café at 214 Commercial Street is open Monday to Friday 7:00–16:00 and weekends 8:00–15:00. Public cuppings run every Saturday at 10:00 and are free — no booking needed.

Shipping

Orders placed before noon Eastern ship the same day. Shipping is free on orders over $45 in the continental US; otherwise it is a flat $6. We do not ship outside the US yet.

Subscriptions

A subscription sends a fresh bag every 1, 2 or 4 weeks and takes 10% off every order. You can pause, skip or cancel from your account at any time, with no minimum commitment.
