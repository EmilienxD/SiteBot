Privacy

Harbor & Pine is a fictional coffee roastery. This page describes the chatbot demo.

Chat information

When you send a message, the chatbot sends your conversation and context from the current page to its server and configured AI provider. With a local provider such as Ollama, model processing runs locally. Conversation history and pending approvals can be stored so you can continue chatting after navigating or reloading the page.

Do not enter passwords, payment details or other sensitive information into this demo.

Using this template on your own site

Replace this demo notice with your site's privacy policy, including the providers you use, what information you store, how long you keep it and how visitors can contact you.
